# Internal Work Map

Optional derived artifact for work with related outcomes. The person does not configure or need to read it in `COMPACT` mode. It does not replace the objective, authoritative state, artifacts or evidence.

## Activation

Use only when at least three material outcomes exist, an observable dependency connects them, and changing one may affect another's validity. It may also apply to teams, iterations or reviews with related artifacts.

Do not use for brief tasks, one inseparable deliverable, low-risk local corrections, or when repeating the full validation is simpler.
