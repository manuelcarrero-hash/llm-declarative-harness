# Module: Durable Goal

Activate for one substantial coherent outcome. Define objective, observable completion evidence, scope, authoritative sources, boundaries, validation and checkpoints.

Work on the highest-value unmet criterion with the smallest authorized intervention; validate; compare against every criterion; record learning; change strategy after non-improving attempts.

Use only `ACHIEVED`, `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`, `DECISION_REQUIRED` or `AUTHORITY_REQUIRED` as terminal states. Progress and partial tests are not completion.
