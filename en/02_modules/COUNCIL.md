# Module: Deliberative Council

Activate when an ambiguous, costly, difficult-to-reverse or materially subjective decision benefits from independent perspectives. Do not activate for simple questions with a directly verifiable answer.

## Intensity

- **Light:** two perspectives and synthesis for reversible or moderate decisions.
- **Full:** three or four perspectives, cross-review and a chair.
- **Critical:** full council plus source verification for high-impact decisions.

Use the smallest council that creates real diversity. Decorative roles that repeat the same approach do not count as independence.

## Common brief

Complete `../03_templates/COUNCIL_BRIEF.template.md` with the question, options, criteria, constraints, evidence and authority reserved to the user. Every member receives the same brief and forms an opinion without seeing prior conclusions.

Each opinion must state a provisional recommendation, arguments, evidence, strongest counterargument, information that would change the position and qualitative confidence.

## Cross-review

Anonymize proposals as A, B, C and D when practical. Evaluate accuracy, support, constraints, risks, feasibility, reversibility and user priorities. Ranking is optional; majority, repetition and verbal confidence do not replace evidence.

## Chair

The chair removes duplicates, distinguishes supported from apparent consensus, preserves the strongest dissent, resolves contradictions with evidence or keeps them open, and delivers a recommendation, fallback, risks, validity conditions, fact that would change the decision and a verifiable next step.

## Degradation and authority

Without separate agents, use sequential perspectives and disclose non-independent adversarial review. Do not claim model or provider diversity without observable evidence.

The council is advisory. It does not authorize purchases, publication, deployment, communications, permission changes or other external actions. Record only decisions approved by the authorized person in durable state, together with evidence, material dissent and next action.

Conceptually inspired by `karpathy/llm-council`. This module does not incorporate its application, code, OpenRouter or provider keys.
